# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Tradesmen, contractors, and technical firms who want to win better jobs, stop dealing with low-quality leads ("tyre-kickers"), and look like top-tier contractors without spending excessive time on marketing or bid decks.

## Product Purpose
A system that gets tradesmen better jobs and helps them win them. It is not a tool directory or a generic SaaS dashboard. It centers around the "Intake Engine" (finds, filters, and sends high-quality opportunities), supported by tools like Vantage, Vicinity, and Codex to transform past work and technical content into high-converting sales assets.

## Brand Personality
Bold, high-contrast, "DeWalt" aesthetic, direct, aggressive, trade-focused, confident. No fluff, no "AI" buzzwords. 

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards with muted colors and rounded glassmorphism
- Bland, corporate directories
- "Prettier firms" that win bids purely on generic templates rather than substance
- Anything that looks like a standard tech startup landing page

## Design Principles
- **Trade-Focused Reality**: Speak directly to the tradesman's pain points (e.g., "Stop quoting for tyre-kickers").
- **Control and Authority**: Make smaller firms look like top-tier contractors to win bigger jobs.
- **Aggressive Clarity**: Cut the fluff, use bold, high-contrast visual elements (Yellow/Navy/Black) to guide the eye.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast must be maintained, especially with the Yellow/Navy/Paper palette.
- Clear, readable typography (Barlow and Barlow Condensed).
- Straightforward, unambiguous copy for all interactive elements.
