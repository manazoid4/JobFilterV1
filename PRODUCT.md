# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Small UK construction, maintenance, and specialist-trade firms capable of bidding directly for public work or approaching a principal contractor as a subcontractor.

## Product Purpose
JobFilter is a public-opportunity qualification layer, not a domestic job marketplace. It reads current Find a Tender notices and helps a small firm decide whether an opportunity fits its trade, location, eligibility, procurement stage, and available time. The output must include source evidence, bid/no-bid reasoning, a deadline, and a practical next action such as bidding directly, watching the notice, or pursuing a subcontract route. Official tenders remain public and may be pursued by other suppliers.

## Current data contract
- **Live/current:** Find a Tender OCDS release packages.
- **Legacy/backfill:** Contracts Finder; never part of the current-notice scan.
- **Experimental:** Planning Data and other unproven adapters; disabled by default and absent from primary customer promises.
- **Credential-required:** EPC, Companies House, delivery, payment, and messaging integrations must not be described as live until configured and verified.

## Brand Personality
Bold, high-contrast, "DeWalt" aesthetic, direct, aggressive, trade-focused, confident. No fluff, no "AI" buzzwords. 

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards with muted colors and rounded glassmorphism
- Bland, corporate directories
- "Prettier firms" that win bids purely on generic templates rather than substance
- Anything that looks like a standard tech startup landing page

## Design Principles
- **Trade-Focused Reality**: Speak to the cost of reading irrelevant tender notices and preparing bids that do not fit.
- **Control and Authority**: Give smaller firms clear evidence and next actions without implying an award, exclusive access, or a no-bid route.
- **Aggressive Clarity**: Cut the fluff, use bold, high-contrast visual elements (Yellow/Navy/Black) to guide the eye.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast must be maintained, especially with the Yellow/Navy/Paper palette.
- Clear, readable typography (Barlow and Barlow Condensed).
- Straightforward, unambiguous copy for all interactive elements.
