# JobFilter Competitor Strategy Playbook

Updated: 2026-06-24  
Scope: repo competitor list plus TradeScale, Copper Lane Communications, Fixt Maintenance, and BAM Renovate / Bricks & Mortar Renovations.

## Objective

Use competitor strengths without turning JobFilter into a bloated all-in-one tool.

JobFilter stays the **intake/filtering foreman** for UK tradesmen:

1. Qualify better jobs faster.
2. Quote and follow up with less admin.
3. Convert more of the right work.
4. Stop wasting time on tyre-kickers, bad-fit jobs, and crowded lead auctions.

Core line:

> REAL LEADS. NO CHASING. NO COMPETING. STAY IN CONTROL.

---

## 1) Market Read

Most competitors fight for one of five jobs:

1. **Generate more enquiries** through websites, SEO, ads, content, and reviews.
2. **Run the job diary** through field-service, CRM, scheduling, invoicing, and ops tools.
3. **Capture missed calls** with AI receptionists and intake scripts.
4. **Speed up quotes** with estimating, templates, and admin shortcuts.
5. **Help homeowners manage work** through renovation planning and maintenance request apps.

The gap is still clear:

> Most tools create more noise or manage work after it is accepted. Few help a tradesman decide if the job is worth touching in the first place.

JobFilter should own that decision point.

## 2) Competitor Landscape

### A) Marketing Agencies / Trade Lead Gen Services

**Existing repo list**

- WeBuildTrades
- Buildmyservice.co.uk
- Timetoscale

**New research**

| Competitor | Positioning | What they sell | JobFilter read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade Scale (`tradescale.cc`) | Marketing systems for trades | Websites, review reactivation, local SEO, more enquiries | Directly relevant. They sell demand generation, not lead filtering. |
| TradeScale (`tradescale.io`) | Marketing agency for trade and service businesses | Brand, website, Google ads, social, lead strategy | Strong pain-aware messaging. They attack weak online presence and shared lead platforms. |
| TradeScale USA (`tradescaleusa.com`) | Online growth for trades and contractors | Google Business Profile, social, reviews, websites, paid ads, content, data, targeted lead research | Broad agency offer. Good menu of growth services, but qualification is not the core. |
| tradescale-marketing.co.uk | Trade marketing support | Websites and growth support | Adjacent UK agency. Needs deeper watch if it gains traction. |

**What they do well**

- Clear trade-specific positioning.
- Sell the outcome trades understand: more calls, booked jobs, stronger local presence.
- Use reviews, Google Business Profile, content, and paid ads as trust engines.
- TradeScale messaging attacks HomeAdvisor-style shared leads, which is close to JobFilter's "NO COMPETING" line.

**Where they are weak**

- More enquiries can mean more bad enquiries.
- Lead quality is usually downstream from the marketing promise.
- Small crews can spend months waiting for SEO/ads to pay back.
- Agencies can become retainers before the tradesman sees control.

**JobFilter counter-position**

- "Marketing gets the phone ringing. JobFilter decides what is worth your time."
- "More leads is not control. Better leads is control."
- "We do not sell you noise. We filter the job before it hits your day."

**Product move**

- Add a simple "source quality" field to each lead: Google, referral, Facebook, directory, intake link, outbound, unknown.
- Score source quality over time by won/lost/no-quote outcomes.
- Build a "bad lead tax" calculator for the homepage or free tool: missed hours x bad quotes x travel x no-shows.

### B) Job Management Platforms (FSM / CRM / Ops)

**Existing repo list**

- tradifyhq.com
- servicebro.io
- Fergus (`fergus.com`)
- Workpal.app
- HaulTechOfficial

**What they do well**

- Scheduling, jobs, invoicing, team workflows, operations stack.
- Good for trades with staff, repeat jobs, and a need for process.
- Strong retention once embedded.

**Where they are weak**

- Heavy onboarding.
- Too broad for solo/small crews who just need faster lead decisions.
- Conversion and qualification are often secondary to admin workflows.

**JobFilter counter-position**

- JobFilter handles the front-door decision layer before full job management.
- Integrate with FSM tools later instead of replacing them.

**Product move**

- Keep "Send to diary / CRM" as an export or integration, not a full job management rebuild.
- Prioritise CSV, email, WhatsApp, and webhook handoff before native deep integrations.

### C) AI Receptionist / AI Call Handling

**Existing repo list**

- Trademore.uk / Checkatrade AI receptionist angle
- Cyberstaff_AI
- TradeHalo

**What they do well**

- Missed-call capture.
- Booking automation.
- Fast response when the tradesman is on site.

**Where they are weak**

- Call capture without hard filtering just creates a bigger inbox.
- Generic scripts can reduce trust if they do not sound trade-specific.
- Voice AI can feel risky for small operators if it misquotes or overpromises.

**JobFilter counter-position**

- "Capture the enquiry, then filter it hard."
- Keep call intake optional and lightweight.
- Do not become a generic receptionist app.

**Product move**

- Missed-call SMS: "Send job details here" intake link.
- Score first, then alert.
- GOLD leads go to WhatsApp. BIN leads stay out of the tradesman's day.

### D) Estimating / Quoting Specialists

**Existing repo list**

- eaziquote
- mybuildestimate.co.uk

**What they do well**

- Faster estimate generation.
- Quote templates and clearer pricing documents.
- Less admin once a job is worth quoting.

**Where they are weak**

- Quote speed does not fix bad lead selection.
- Users still spend effort on wrong-fit jobs.

**JobFilter counter-position**

- "Do not quote everything faster. Quote the right jobs faster."

**Product move**

- Smart Quote starter only appears for SILVER and GOLD leads.
- Show reason codes before quote copy so the tradesman knows what risk to price in.

### F) Homeowner Renovation Planning Apps

**New research**

| Competitor | Positioning | Pricing / notes | JobFilter read |
|---|---|---|---|

**What they do well**

- Strong "stay in control" language for homeowners.
- Budgeting, planning, document storage, structured scope, and expert guidance.
- Clear pricing.
- The product teaches homeowners how to compare quotes and challenge bad work.

**Where they are weak**

- Built for homeowners, not trades.
- Could make homeowners more organised, but also more demanding.
- Does not solve tradesman lead qualification unless it routes quality jobs to trades.

**JobFilter counter-position**

- "Homeowners need control over the renovation. Trades need control over the work coming in."
- "JobFilter is the tradesman's side of that control."

**Product move**

- Add a "customer preparedness" signal:
  - has photos,
  - has scope,
  - has budget,
  - has timeframe,
  - owns the property / decision maker,
  - has access details.
- This turns homeowner organisation into a lead quality score, not just admin notes.

## 3) Competitive White Space

JobFilter can own a sharper position than the agencies, FSM tools, AI receptionists, and homeowner apps:

> The control layer between "someone wants work done" and "a tradesman spends time on it."

That means:

- Lead scoring before quoting.
- Trade-specific intake before admin.
- Reason codes before phone calls.
- WhatsApp alerts only when the job is worth attention.
- Follow-up only when the lead is worth saving.
- Quotes only after the job passes the filter.

Do not compete on "all-in-one".

Compete on:

- **speed to decision**
- **bad lead rejection**
- **no competing**
- **less chasing**
- **more control**

## 5) What JobFilter Must Avoid

1. Becoming a generic all-in-one suite.
2. Selling lead volume instead of lead quality.
3. Building a full agency service before the product is tight.
4. Building full FSM features before handoff/export proves demand.
5. Adding voice AI before intake scoring is trusted.
6. Copying homeowner planning apps and confusing the buyer.
7. Hiding the score logic. Trades need to know why a job is GOLD, SILVER, BRONZE, or BIN.

## 6) Product Moves Ranked

### Build Now

1. **Lead Quality Score + Reason Codes**
   - GOLD >=80, SILVER >=50, BRONZE 30-49, BIN <30.
   - Show distance risk, budget risk, urgency, scope clarity, photos, and source.

2. **Trade-Specific Intake Profiles**
   - Electrician, plumber, builder, heating, roofing, landscaping.
   - Defaults for job types, travel radius, minimum budget, urgent jobs.

3. **Customer Preparedness Score**
   - Scope written clearly.
   - Photos/video attached.
   - Budget present.
   - Timeline present.
   - Decision maker confirmed.
   - Access / postcode present.

4. **Smart Quote Fast-Start Packs**
   - Only for SILVER and GOLD.
   - Include risk notes from the score.

5. **Review Harvester**
   - Simple follow-up after completed jobs.
   - Google review link.
   - One-click WhatsApp/SMS copy.

### Build Next

6. **Missed-Call to Intake Capture**
   - Text back an intake link.
   - Score before alerting the tradesman.

7. **Quote-Win Feedback Loop**
   - Won / lost / no quote / no show.
   - Feed back into source and job-type scoring.

8. **Weekly Foreman Digest**
   - Jobs filtered.
   - Quotes sent.
   - Payments chased.
   - Reviews requested.
   - Best source by win rate.

### Integrate Later

9. **FSM / CRM Export**
   - CSV, email, webhook first.
   - Tradify/Fergus/Workpal later if users ask.

10. **Marketing Source Attribution Lite**
   - Do not become an agency dashboard.
   - Show which sources create GOLD jobs, not just enquiries.
