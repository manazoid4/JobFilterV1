## DIRECT COMPETITORS (Lead Gen / Directories)

### 1. Checkatrade (checkatrade.com)
- **Model:** Consumer-facing trade directory + review platform
- **Pricing:** £100–£200+/mo, notorious for massive renewal price hikes (185% increases reported)
- **Strengths:** Massive brand awareness, dominant SEO, TV advertising, £1,000 guarantee
- **Weaknesses:** Reactive (wait for phone), 14-day cancel trap then locked for a year, lead saturation, trades resent renewal hikes
- **JobFilter edge:** Proactive leads before jobs are advertised, £39/mo flat, exclusive territory
- **Attack line:** "Stop fighting 5 other plumbers for the same job after Checkatrade hikes your renewal fee again."
- **Content angle:** "Checkatrade renewal fees 2026: Are trades finally walking away?"

### 2. Bark.com (bark.com)
- **Model:** Generic pay-per-lead marketplace (dog walkers to electricians)
- **Pricing:** Credits system, £5–£30+ per lead, credits expire in 3 months
- **Strengths:** Massive volume across all categories, slick UI
- **Weaknesses:** Low-intent leads, leads sold to multiple pros, expiring credits, "99% don't respond" complaint
- **JobFilter edge:** Exclusive scored leads, no credits, flat £39/mo
- **Attack line:** "Don't buy expired credits just to message customers who will ghost you anyway."
- **Content angle:** "Bark vs MyBuilder vs JobFilter: Which leads actually answer the phone?"

### 3. MyBuilder (mybuilder.com)
- **Model:** Job-posting board, trades pay shortlist fee when selected for quote
- **Pricing:** Free to join, £2–£35+ per shortlist depending on job size
- **Strengths:** Trades only pay when customer selects them, strong homeowner adoption
- **Weaknesses:** Race to bottom on price, £100-300 spend before first won job is normal, <17% win rate, no recourse
- **JobFilter edge:** No bidding, exclusive leads, flat fee
- **Attack line:** "Stop spending your evenings writing pitches just to lose out to the cheapest quote."
- **Content angle:** "The hidden cost of MyBuilder shortlist fees for solo trades."

### 4. Rated People (ratedpeople.com)
- **Model:** Pay-per-lead directory, similar to MyBuilder
- **Pricing:** ~£15-£20/mo subscription + £5-£50+ per lead (shared with up to 3 trades)
- **Strengths:** Brand recognition, mobile app
- **Weaknesses:** Shared leads (up to 3 trades), support replaced by AI chatbot, 2-month wait for tickets, fees doubled
- **JobFilter edge:** Exclusive leads, human support, flat £39/mo
- **Attack line:** "Paying £30 for a lead is painful when three other trades are already calling them."
- **Content angle:** "Getting exclusive trade leads in 2026: Rated People alternatives."

### 5. TrustATrader (trustatrader.com)
- **Model:** Pure-play review and directory site, proof of qualifications required
- **Pricing:** ~£600-£1,000/year fixed annual fee
- **Strengths:** Zero per-lead fees, limited memberships per area, high consumer trust
- **Weaknesses:** Completely reactive, passive directory, just waiting for phone to ring
- **JobFilter edge:** Proactive WhatsApp delivery, scored leads, £39/mo
- **Attack line:** "A directory listing is just a digital yellow pages; stop waiting for the phone to ring."
- **Content angle:** "Is TrustATrader worth the annual fee in 2026?"

## PLANNING DATA COMPETITORS

### 6. BuildAlert (buildalert.co.uk)
- **Model:** Planning data lead gen with physical letter delivery
- **Pricing:** Standard £50/mo (25 letters), Business £100/mo (50 letters), Premium £150/mo (75 letters)
- **Strengths:** Direct mail effective for older demographics, bypasses digital noise
- **Weaknesses:** Slow delivery (3 days post), physical only, no scoring, no WhatsApp, expensive vs digital
- **JobFilter edge:** WhatsApp delivery day 1, scored leads, £39/mo
- **Attack line:** "By the time your letter arrives, a JobFilter user has already quoted the job."
- **Content angle:** "Automating construction planning leads: Direct Mail vs WhatsApp delivery."

### 7. BuildScout (buildscout.co.uk) — DIRECT COMPETITOR
- **Model:** UK construction lead gen from planning portals, targeting SME builders/contractors
- **Pricing:** Standard £249+VAT/mo, Growth £299+VAT/mo, Pro £349+VAT/mo (FMB/FCA members only)
- **Strengths:** Deep planning data, document text search, architect details, property value data, FMB partnership
- **Weaknesses:** 6-9x more expensive than JobFilter, physical letters (£1+VAT each), no WhatsApp, no anti-ghost scoring, no territory exclusivity, FMB members only
- **JobFilter edge:** £39/mo vs £249-349/mo, WhatsApp delivery, exclusive territories, anti-ghost scoring, EPC data fusion, sole-trader friendly
- **Attack line:** "BuildScout charges you £249-349/month to send expensive physical letters to the same planning leads JobFilter WhatsApps you for £39/month with territory exclusivity."
- **Content angle:** "BuildScout vs JobFilter: Planning data platforms compared for solo trades."
- **Threat level:** HIGH — could pivot downmarket if funded

### 8. Planning Pipe (planningpipe.co.uk)
- **Model:** Online portal for early-stage construction project info from planning applications
- **Pricing:** Regional (2 regions) £44.95/mo, National £89.95/mo
- **Strengths:** Affordable vs enterprise, decent planning data baseline
- **Weaknesses:** Old-school delivery (spreadsheets/web portal), no WhatsApp, no scoring, no territory exclusivity
- **JobFilter edge:** WhatsApp delivery, scored leads, exclusive territories, £39/mo
- **Attack line:** "Spreadsheets are for accountants; get your leads sent straight to your phone via WhatsApp."
- **Content angle:** "Planning Pipe reviews: Is there a better way to get planning leads?"

## SECONDARY / EMERGING COMPETITORS

### 11. Dean Keating (deankeating.com)
- **Model:** SEO websites for tradesmen, WhatsApp-first sales
- **Pricing:** From £59/mo (websites), £197/mo (Google Maps ranking), £197/mo (Facebook Ads)
- **Strengths:** Anti-Checkatrade positioning, WhatsApp-first sales approach
- **Weaknesses:** One-man operation, no data moat, SEO takes months, no verified reviews
- **JobFilter edge:** Immediate leads day one vs months of SEO, planning data moat, £39/mo
- **Attack line:** "Dean Keating sells you a website and hopes Google ranks it — JobFilter delivers actual planning leads tomorrow morning for less money."
- **Threat level:** LOW

### 12. Unmissed AI (unmissed.ai)
- **Model:** Missed call recovery and AI lead capture for trades
- **Pricing:** Starter £49/mo (100 conversations), Unlimited £79/mo, free 10 conversations
- **Strengths:** Mobile-first, trade-specific questions, instant callback
- **Weaknesses:** Only catches inbound leads you already attracted, limited to South Wales currently
- **JobFilter edge:** Proactive lead generation vs reactive call capture
- **Attack line:** "Unmissed catches the calls you miss — JobFilter finds the jobs you'd never have known about."
- **Threat level:** MEDIUM — could add lead gen features
- **Partnership opportunity:** Complementary (JobFilter generates, Unmissed catches)

### 13. KlipBoard (klipboard.io)
- **Model:** UK field service management (FSM) software
- **Pricing:** Starter £28-34/mo per user (min 2 users, 12-24mo contracts), Innovator £48-58/mo per user

- **Weaknesses:** Requires 2+ users, long contracts, manages existing jobs not finding new ones
- **JobFilter edge:** Lead generation vs job management, sole-trader friendly, no contract
- **Attack line:** "KlipBoard manages the jobs you've already won — JobFilter finds you the jobs you haven't even seen yet."
- **Threat level:** LOW

### 14. Spruce (spruce.eco)

## MARKET SUMMARY 2026

- **Direct competitor count:** ~10 major players in two camps: reactive directories (Checkatrade, MyBuilder, Bark, Rated People) and planning data platforms (BuildScout, BuildAlert, Planning Pipe)
- **New entrants since May 2026:** None identified. No AI-powered UK trade lead gen tools launched Jan 2025-July 2026 competing with JobFilter's model
- **Biggest pricing shifts:** Checkatrade renewal hikes continue (up to 185%), Bark credits now expire in 3 months, BuildScout solidified at £249-349/mo
- **#1 unmet pain point:** Race to the bottom + ghosting. Shared directories force price wars; low-intent leads waste money
- **JobFilter's unoccupied position:** Proactive, Mobile-first, Affordable Intelligence. Enterprise-grade planning + EPC data delivered via WhatsApp at £39/mo with territory exclusivity. The sub-£50/mo data intelligence space is completely empty.
